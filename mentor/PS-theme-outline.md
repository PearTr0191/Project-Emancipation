## 5. Guardrails (non-negotiables)

1. **Engineer's lens budget:** read receipt (open) + ViDrive spec (close) + AT MOST 1–2 light touches between. No metaphor stacking ("unhandled exception," "garbage collection," etc. — banned).
2. **Journal quoting:** max 1–2 lines total. Best candidates: *"I grow little of the praises, grieve much of the failures"* / *"how far I am from everyone, no matter direction."*
3. **Banned register (resentment narrative):** "people who will break it," "prove everyone wrong," establishment/destiny grievance framing. Privately true; on the page it pattern-matches risk.
4. **No whole-journal-as-essay:** all internals, no arc. It's a quarry, not a building.
5. **Weight on forward motion:** what he DOES about the gap (mates, tools, translation), never how bad the gap feels. No imposter-syndrome spiral endings.
6. **Voice:** keep his journal cadence — aphoristic, slightly formal ("My eyes, always towards... My heart, to..."). Don't sand down into generic polish.
7. **Authenticity policy (2026 cycle):** raw material must stay his. AI-written prose actively hurts; this outline is scaffolding, the sentences must come from him.
8. **Emotion ≠ absence:** the essay must include Movement 2, or it falsifies him into the emotionless-quant stereotype.
9. **Family material routing (from §7.4):** the lineage — grandfather (fled Quang Tri bloodbath to Ha Tinh, two fingers lost to bomb fragments), father (bare-hands rise to consulting founder/VP, gave it up to raise the children; financial literacy taught without capital) — belongs in the **why-major supplement, recommender one-pager, and Additional Info**, NOT crammed into the PS. Max ONE clause in the PS if any.
10. **Family framing rules:** mother is never characterized negatively in any application text ("middling," frugality complaints stay out). Father ambivalence must land on respect + correction, never blame — "I seek not to repeat his mistake" yes; "his mistake" as grievance no. Household financial specifics go in aid forms/context sections factually, not essays.
11. **Cultural observation rule:** describe INSTANCES, never nations. "At that table the conversation died after two questions" — fine. "Vietnamese social culture is hollow/not exploitable" — banned. US readers punish cultural verdicts and reward specific scenes; also this isn't uniquely Vietnamese (performative pep rallies and test small talk exist in every US high school), so the indictment fails on facts anyway.
12. **"See-through" register ban:** the I-alone-perceive-the-facade posture never appears in the PS. Every 17-year-old analyst writes that essay; readers have seen thousands. The honest version is "I misread what the rituals were FOR" — misreading is his to fix, which converts critique into forward motion.
11. **Culture-critique ban (2026-08-26 evening):** No framing of Vietnamese peers/culture as fake, hypocritical, "all-noise," or emotionally "self-induced/exaggerated." Claims about others' inner falseness are unwinnable and read as contempt — the classic "I'm not like my peers" rejection pattern. Every such observation must be OWNED as protocol mismatch: their scripts deliver real connection *for them*; my latency is the gap. Never "I saw through them"; always "I couldn't decode them."
