# Timelines, Checklists & Deadlines

## Grade-by-grade priorities

**9th grade** — Build the foundation: craft a value-based theme early; admissions officers look for a value-based spike built over years. [C-Nqs58MkWe]

**10th grade** — Consistency and depth year. Start early; distinguish competitive vs passion-driven activities; build the Tier-2 foundation. [C-GFEWYuYfL, DA05GCQusmD]

**11th grade (the real work year)** — three moves:
1. Research colleges now → fit clarity feeds why-us essays and interviews later
2. Reflect on and curate extracurriculars: drop what doesn't fit your goals; double down on leadership; start the theme-anchoring project
3. Build real relationships with junior-year teachers — they write your recommendation letters; make those letters easy to write [DIBoicsMYY3]

**12th grade** — execution: essays, supplements, interviews, LOCI if deferred/waitlisted. Starting prep in senior year is already late. [DIBoicsMYY3]

## Summer before senior year — catch-up checklist

If you haven't started by summer, you're behind. Minimum:
1. **Choose the application theme** (unifies all essays)
2. **Draft the personal statement** (early = revision time)
3. **Identify recommenders**: one academic + one personal who truly know you
4. **Research schools deeply** for supplementals and interviews [C82DnKrM_Lm]

## Seasonal rhythm notes

- January/February (junior year): plan the year; she flags February as an overlooked month that "decides" outcomes for organized students [DUOsaLLgJiG]
- Late January: 4-week window that decides whether summer programs align with your application — mark calendars for program application openings [DTNkktNAXGF]
- August (rising senior): monthly application checklist exists ("August college application checklist for rising seniors") [Dbd-hU7FDXb]
- Application season opens ~Aug 1; ED/EA deadlines ~Nov 1 (standard calendar assumed in her senior content)

## Waitlist / deferral playbook

A deferral or waitlist is not over. The lever is the **Letter of Continued Interest** (LOCI), not new achievements:
- Don't scramble to add accomplishments or compete with peers
- Use her 6-step LOCI framework (framework details sold separately in source; core principle from case study: one focused letter turned a UCLA waitlist into an acceptance for a student without strong stats) [DDr7K4EvESr]

Principles for a strong LOCI consistent with the corpus: restate commitment (if ED/waitlist, it's still #1), update with specific new developments since applying, connect them explicitly to the school's programs, keep it short and specific.

## Deadline-season triage

During deadline windows, lead with timeline triage: what's due first, what has dependencies (recommender lead times, test score sending), what can be batched.

- **Michigan postpone mechanics** (RD-fallback relevant): Michigan postpones early applicants in bulk instead of denying; a postpone = the file is alive and they want more evidence — new SAT/ACT + first-term senior grades until Feb 1 (they invite this in writing; almost nobody does it), plus the Expression of Continued Interest form in Enrollment Connect due March 1.
