# School List & "Why Us" Strategy

## Institutional priorities — the hidden variable

Each year, admissions officers set priorities for the upcoming cycle; students matching key institutional priorities get an advantage. Schools don't publish them, but **you can infer what a college evaluates by reading its supplemental essay prompts** — prompts reveal what the institution currently values. [DBTvY_ZOa5m]

Practical use: before finalizing the list or writing supplements, mine each school's prompts for emphasis (community? intellectual curiosity? service? innovation?) and check your theme's fit.

## Research phase timing

College research belongs in **junior year**, not senior year:
- Figuring out school fit early gives you a strong base for "why us" essays AND interviews
- Senior-year research under deadline pressure produces generic essays [DIBoicsMYY3]

## Why-us answers that work

The rejected/waitlisted/accepted pattern for school-specific questions (works for both essays and interviews):
- ❌ Rejected: generic praise — "NYU is in NYC with internships." The school name is swappable.
- ⚠️ Waitlisted: aware of setting but abstract motivation ("real-world experiences," "interdisciplinary") without personal anchoring.
- ✅ Accepted: **past → present → future**: a concrete personal experience tied to the school, a clear academic direction aligned with it, and a specific way you'll engage with that environment.

Don't explain why the school is impressive; explain why *your story unfolds differently there than anywhere else*. [DTwVRxGkr-e]

## Demonstrated interest & list construction

- Deep-dive each school's uniqueness to tailor supplementals and interviews [C82DnKrM_Lm]
- Fit runs both ways: match your theme to what the school prioritizes when deciding where it goes on the list (reach/target/safety framing appears across posts but exact list-size rules were not captured in this corpus)
