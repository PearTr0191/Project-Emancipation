## Theme-to-evidence mapping (for quick reference during drafting)

- **Theme:** The 5% Gap — consistently ~5% out of sync. Not absence of feeling; asynchronous emotion (latency). The gap isn't a flaw — it's the work.
- **Controlling image:** The read receipt (ACK in code / wound between people).
- **Core value:** Translation — closing the gap, not the gap itself.
- **M1 (public):** OGL bonding event — the moment logic runs out, someone steps in.
- **Seam (user):** 30M VND saved; logic gave a stranger's instinct a floor.
- **M2 (private):** CircleK (1540, score changes nothing) + IELTS/VTV (national television for two weeks, respect yes / trust no / love no; classmate detail removed 2026-08-31).
- **Close:** ViDrive spec (5% error); no tolerance available for himself.
- **Journal quotes used (max 2 per guardrail):** *"I grow little of the praises, grieve much of the failures"* (not in PS draft, but is a best candidate); *"how far I am from everyone, no matter direction"* (used in M2); *"My eyes, always towards those at the very top... My heart, to the people who I know will break it"* (used in hook).
- **Family material (guardrails 9-10):** routed to why-major supplement + recommender one-pager + Additional Info. Stays out of PS. Father: respect + correction. Mother: never negative.
- **Culture-critique ban:** no "fake peers" framing. Every observation owned as protocol mismatch: "their scripts deliver real connection for them; my latency is the gap."
- **Banned phrases (guardrail 3):** "people who will break it" (dropped from M2), "prove everyone wrong" (cut from journal quote in M2), establishment/destiny grievance framing (the journal's *"What version of reality did the establishment serve me?"* stays out of the essay entirely — true, but reads as grievance).
- **See-through ban (guardrail 12):** never "I alone perceive" — replaced with "I misread what the rituals were for," which converts critique into forward motion.
- **Register budget:** read receipt (hook, open) + ViDrive spec (close, terminate) + 1 touch between. No "unhandled exception" or "garbage collection" metaphors.
- **Authenticity (guardrail 7):** raw material stays his. M2 uses his actual journal lines. The hook borrows his own *"My eyes / My heart"* language. The close is his own sentence, refined.
- **Emotion != absence (guardrail 8):** M2 is included; without it, the essay falsifies him into the emotionless-quant stereotype.
- **Trim hierarchy:** 1) seam shrinks; 2) user details drop; 3) hook ornament cut; 4) NEVER cut M2 spine or close.
