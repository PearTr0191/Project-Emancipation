## Aid posture by school — the two separate questions (Stanford source-verified 2026-10-02)

**"Need-blind" and "meets 100% of need" are different gates.** Need-blind means finances cannot affect the *admit decision*. Meets-full-need means that *once admitted*, the school covers your demonstrated need. At $7.5k/yr the student needs **both** — a school that meets full need but is need-aware still lets a low-income international get rejected on affordability.

So the stretch **downgrades VinUni's requirement from "top scholarship only" to "Dean's Distinction or above."** Given VinUni is his highest-odds near-zero-cost path, that is where the flexibility is worth something. Apply as if $30k; treat $40k as upside that lowers the domestic scholarship bar, not as license to re-add US schools.

**Why the swap (decision record 2026-09-21):**
- **Stanford's probability is roughly constant REA vs RD.** No published separate REA rate; REA pools skew recruited athletes/hooked applicants; Stanford itself says REA is not designed to inflate odds. For an unhooked full-need international the REA read ≈ an RD read, just earlier — and need-aware for internationals means the early read would happen where need hurts.
- **The Dartmouth read strictly improves.** Need-blind for internationals (verified 2026-09) so need cannot factor; ED pool carries a generally real admit-rate advantage; 1540 is above Dartmouth's median composite (CDS) and below Stanford's median.
- **NYU ED is dominated by Dartmouth ED for this profile.** NYU is need-aware for internationals (Promise guarantees aid IF admitted; need can hurt the admit itself), and ED I would bind the student to their #2 option at ~9% RD-tier odds. Dartmouth is need-blind, the #1 pick, and the ED slot costs nothing extra.
- **ED lock-in risk minimized.** Need-blind + full-need removes the "admitted but unaffordable" trap. Verified 2026-09-21 from Dartmouth Admissions: (1) ED is binding — withdraw all pending applications if admitted, make no new applications anywhere; (2) aid-seeking ED candidates need not withdraw other applications until the aid notification arrives; (3) explicit release clause — if aid makes enrollment impossible, "the College will release the admitted student from their commitment to enroll" ("only a handful" released in recent years).
- **Public EAs stay dropped.** Non-binding public EA remains permitted under Dartmouth ED, but Michigan/GT/Purdue have no 80%+ scholarship path at $7.5k/yr (confirmed 2026-09-21).
- **Honesty note:** Dartmouth's ED pool still skews hooked; the unhooked-international advantage is real but smaller than headline ED-vs-RD gaps imply. The file still has to earn the admit.

**Superseded history:** the 2026-09-01 slate (Stanford REA + Michigan EA + GT EA II + Purdue EA) was invalidated in two steps — the 2026-09-07 budget drop to $7.5k/yr removed the publics (no full-need for internationals, no co-op = unbacked debt), and the 2026-09-21 swap moved Stanford to RD so Dartmouth could take the binding early slot.
