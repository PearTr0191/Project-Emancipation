## Slide text

- slide_01: Factors 1-5 (based on zip code) Housing Vacancy: Percentage of homes that are abandoned or unoccupied. Rental vs. Ownership: Ratio of families who rent versus those who own their homes. Crime Rate: Local crime statistics compared to national and state averages. Language Barriers: Percentage of households where English is not the primary language. Public Assistance: Percentage of households receiving food stamps (SNAP) or other aid.
- slide_02: Factors 6-10 (based on school) * Senior Class Size: Total number of students in your graduating year. * Locale Type: Whether your school is classified as Rural, Suburban, or Urban. * Teacher Turnover: Stability of the teaching staff and faculty. * Counselor Ratio: Number of students per guidance counselor. * Historical Admit Data: Data on how students from your school have fared in past admissions cycles at that specific college.
