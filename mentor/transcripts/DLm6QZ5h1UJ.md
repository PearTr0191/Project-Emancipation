## Caption

If your application doesn’t tell a clear story, admissions officers won’t remember you…

Before you hit submit, go through this full checklist to make sure your app actually has a strong narrative strategy 👇

✅ Do you have a core theme?
→ A personal value, passion, or goal that connects your story across the app. (Example: curiosity, justice, health equity, innovation, etc.)

✅ Does your personal statement clearly show how that theme was shaped by a defining experience?
→ You should show personal growth and connect it to your future direction.

✅ Do your top 2–3 extracurriculars directly support that theme?
→ They should show depth, initiative, and a clear throughline.

✅ Are your supplemental essays expanding on your theme, not just repeating it?
→ Each essay should add a new dimension: identity, specific interests, future goals, etc.

✅ Did you choose recommenders who can authentically speak to the character traits behind your theme?
→ (Ex: if your theme is leadership through service, your letter should mention that.)

✅ Does your Activities List reflect growth and consistency around your theme?
→ Use strong action verbs and tie roles back to your larger story.

✅ Does your Additional Info Section fill in gaps without distracting from your core narrative?
→ Only include context or relevant side stories that strengthen your theme.

🎯 Last check: if an admissions officer only had 5 minutes to read your app…
Would they walk away knowing exactly what matters to you, what drives you, and where you’re headed?
