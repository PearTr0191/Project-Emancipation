- slide_01: HOW TO = WRITE THE 2 HARVARD + SUPPLEMENTAL ESSAYS
- slide_02: 1. How will the life experiences that shape who you are today enable you to contribute to Harvard? This is diversity loophole essay. They use this to build a diverse class rather that be through race, diversity of thought (political opinion), or diversity of religion etc. Write about the area that makes you most unique
- slide_03: 2. Describe a time when you strongly disagreed with someone about an idea or issue. How did you communicate or engage with this person? What did you learn from this experience? This is an essay to see if you can adjust your form of thinking and fit the liberal arts mold. Give a concrete specific example for this prompt
- slide_04: 3. Briefly describe any of your extracurricular activities, employment experience, travel, or family responsibilities that have shaped who you are. For this essay write about an activity you did not mention in your other essays. Make sure to write ina format where the AO wants to cheer for the student and hope that student contributes something similar to the campus if admitted
