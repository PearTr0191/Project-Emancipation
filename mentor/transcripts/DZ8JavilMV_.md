What makes a good essay hook?
Curiosity: It should make the reader want to keep reading.
Resonance: It connects through emotion, memory, or
conflict.
Individuality: It should sound like something only you would
say.
Smooth Transition: It naturally leads into your story.
Precision: Every word should pull its weight.
What makes a bad essay hook?
Boring: It's generic, overused, or predictable.
Artificial: It sounds dramatic,fake, or uses overly
complicatedwordstosoundsmart.
Detached: It feels random or unrelated.

Vivid Imagery Hook
Accepted
"The street vendors were yelling again. I held my
breath and tried not to step on the cracks."
Why it works:
Uses sensory details and physical actions to place the
reader directly into the scene.
Rejected
"It was a busy street and there were many people
around me."
Why it doesn't work:
It only tells the setting but doesn't visualize it clearly.
