Tip#3:
Plan Your Academics in Reverse
Look at the rigorous classes you want to take junior year, then
work backward to figure out what you need to take now tobe
eligible for them.
Choose your classes that align to your intended
major. Example: Take Honors Chemistry in 10th
grade if you want AP Chemistry in 11th.
Build strong study habits now. Good systems
make challenging classes easier to manage.
Start light sAT prep now so you're ready for the
PSAT in junior year and have a shot at qualifying
forNational Merit.
Remember:Junioryeargradesmatter mostfor college apps.
