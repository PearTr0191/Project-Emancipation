## Caption

The Center for World University Rankings dropped their 2026 list on June 1st and the top 10 looks familiar with three shifts worth noting 👇

- Penn moved up to 7 from last edition’s 8.
- Columbia got bumped down a spot to 8.
- Chicago is back in the top 10 after Caltech took its place in 2024.
- 
CWUR is the largest academic ranking out there and pulls from objective outcomes across four factors:

Research performance: 40%
Quality of education: 25%
Alumni employability: 25%
Quality of faculty: 10%

But one thing this ranking doesn’t measure is how the student experience actually feels.
