## Slide text

- slide_01: IVY BROTHERS HOW TO WRITE THE PRINCETON ESSAYS - ADMISSION OFFICER INSIDER
- slide_02: Academic Track Prompts 1) Question: What academic areas most pique your curiosity, and how do the programs offered at Princeton suit your particular interests? Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): This is a hybrid why major and why college question. You can write here the origin story of your major and what accumulated to the moment of wanting to study it Then you can mention unique programs that students in your major pursue or do during the school year. The more unique the better.
- slide_03: Academic Track Prompts 2) Question: Please describe why you are interested in studying engineering at Princeton. Include any of your experiences in, or exposure to engineering, and how you think the programs offered at the University suit your particular interests. Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): This is similar to the prompt for AB students, don’t just list professors or classes here, look into the yearly activties/trips/engagements engineering students in your chosen department do. Colleges want to see you did a lot of research on their university
- slide_04: Main Essays 1) Question: As prospective member of this community, reflect on how yout lived experiences will impact the conversations you will have in the classroom, the dining hall or other campus spaces. What lessons have you learned in life thus far? What will your classmates learn from you? In short, how has your lived experience shaped you? Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): This is the diversity essay. You can mention your demographics or diversity in thought here too
- slide_05: Main Essays 2) Question: Princeton has a longstanding commitment to understanding our responsibility to society through service and civic engagement. How does your own story intersect with these ideals? Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): This is the essay where you will mention how you'll change the world. You can also mention projects or initiatives you’ve worked on to showcase you love to make change, make sure it relates to your spike
- slide_06: Short Answers 1) Question: What is a new skill you would like to learn in college? Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): Write here a skill that builds upon your intellectual curiosity narrative
- slide_07: Short Answers 2) Question: What brings you joy? Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): Admission officers want to know here what you’re passionate about and what drives you
- slide_08: Short Answers 3) Question: What song represents the soundtrack of your life at this moment? Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): Any song that is very meaningful to you works well here. Briefly explain the rationale too
