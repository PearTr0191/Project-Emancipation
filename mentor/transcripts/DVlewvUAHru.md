## Caption

Most students think a “Why Us?” essay is about praising the school 😬

It’s not.

The difference between a rejected, waitlisted, and accepted essay usually comes down to one thing: trajectory.

Here’s why each response got its result: 

❌ Rejected: This type of answer is just compliments about prestige, community, and campus life. The problem is that every top school could say the same thing. 

⚠️ Waitlisted: Now the student starts naming specific Yale opportunities, which shows they actually researched the school. But the essay still focuses mostly on what Yale offers them rather than what they will do with those opportunities.

✅ Accepted: The strongest answers connect three things clearly: past work → Yale resources → future impact. Instead of just saying they like Yale, the student shows how something they’ve already built will grow through Yale’s academic structure.

That’s what makes a “Why Us?” essay convincing.
