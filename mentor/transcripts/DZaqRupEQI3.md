#1
Unconventional
RealityHook
Think of an ordinary object or idea that might be
strange but holds deep meaning to you. This is
best for Metaphor-heavy essays.

#2
Vivid Imagery
Hook
Let them see, hear, feel, smell, or taste it to make
them feel like they're in that moment with you.
This is a great way to show how your brain works
when you're doing something.
Examples

---

Vivid Imagery Hook
1."The bitter tang of chlorine clung to my hair
as I pressed my goggles tighter, waiting for
the horn"
2."The street vendors were yelling again. I held
my breath and tried not to step on the
cracks."
3."Newly xeroxed papers, cut-out pictures
from a book, dried-up glue on a sticky note,
and my aunt sitting on a children's chair
under the glow of a single lamp.
4.“Smoke drifted out of the machine-I failed
again."
5."The light blinded me for a moment, then
warmed my skin, steadying me as I stepped
into performance."

#3
DialogueHook
Words that were actually spoken: lessons you
learned, passions that shaped you, or someone
who played a meaningful role in your life.
This is best for stories where you want to focus on
your emotional growth.
Examples

---

Dialogue Hook
1."He doesn't talk to anyone anymore. My
aunt changed the subject, but the silence
lingered."
2."1 didn't know it then, but this would be the
last time I looked at that house the same
way.'
3. “It's so painful," my aunt cried, clutching her
shoulder as the leaf pressed against it did
nothing to ease the pain.
4."1, 2, 3, Go!" the race started, and my body
moved quickly.
5. “We're ready," we said in unison, our voices
steady but our hands shaking.
