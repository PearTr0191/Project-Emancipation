- slide_01: 2/6 DELETED, 2023-25 The old prompt is gone. Good, it was lazy anyway. For years the question was blunt: describe your background, your identity, what makes you diverse. Fill in the blank, admissions gives you credit After the 2023 affirmative action ruling, schools quietly pulled that exact wording. NYU had it. So did half the lvy League The Supreme Court did not ban talking about race. It banned crediting you just for having it. “l am X” stopped being an answer on its own So the prompt had to get smarter. - slide_02: ¢ 3/6 NYU / BRIDGE BUILDER DARTMOUTH / “DIFFERENCE” COLUMBIA / DISAGREEMENT COLUMBIA / ADVERSITY Here is what replaced it. Same question, new outfit. NYU now asks for a “bridge builder,” someone who connects people and ideas across a divide Dartmouth asks how “difference has been part of your life” and how you have made it part of your identity Columbia asks you to describe a disagreement you navigated, and separately, a time you faced real adversity None of these say the word identity. All four are built to hold the exact same answer the old prompt wanted
