#1
CSS
A form that some colleges use to learn
about your family's finances.
NOTE: Not every college requires it. Check
your college's financial aid page.
#2
FAFSA
FreeApplicationforFederalStudentAid.It
helps you find out what financial aid you
may qualify for.
NOTE: Your FAFSA information is sent to
the colleges you list on your application.

---

#3
Need-Based Aid
Financial aid given based on how much
financial help your family needs.
NOTE: Colleges may use different rules to
decide how much you qualify for.
#4
Need-Blind Admission
A college policy where your ability to pay
does not affect its admission decision.
NOTE: Check each college's policy
because it may not apply to every student.

---

#5
Supplemental Essay
An extra essay that a college asks you to
write.
NOTE: These often ask why you like the
collegeor aboutanexperiencethatmatters
to you.
#6
Personal Statement
Your main college essay about who you are,
what you've experienced, or what matters
to you.
NOTE: This is the main essay you submit
with your college application.

---

#7
Demonstrated Interest
Things you do to show a college that you
are interested in attending.
NOTE:Many admissions committees
considerthisfactorinmakingtheirdecision.
#8
Transcript
An official record of the classesyou took
andthegradesyouearned.
NOTE: Colleges usuallyreview your grades
from 9thgrade through the most recent
semesteravailable.

---

#9
Early Action
An optionthat letsyou apply to acollege
early and get your decision early.
NOTE: Early Action is usually not binding, so
you do not have to attend if you are
accepted.
#10
eee
Early Decision
An option that letsyou apply early to your
top-choice college and agree to attend if
youareaccepted.
NOTE: Early Decision is binding, meaning
you agree to attend if admitted, subject to
the college's terms.

#11
Academic Rigor
How challenging your classes are
compared with what your school offers.
NOTE: Taking AP,IB,honors,or other
advanced classes can show academic rigor.
#12
Holistic Admissions
Aprocesswherecollegesconsidermultiple
parts of your application, not just your
grades and test scores.
NOTE: This can includeyour activities,
essays, recommendations, and other parts
ofyourapplication.
