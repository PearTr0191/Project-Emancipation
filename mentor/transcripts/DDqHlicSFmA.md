## Slide text

- slide_01: HOW TO WRITE THE YALE SUPPLEMENTAL ESSAYS
- slide_02: 1.. As of this moment, what academic areas seem to fit your interests or goals most comfortably? For this essay, you need storytelling to illustrate the origin of your interest in a particular major and that origin’s alignment with your future plans
- slide_03: 2. Tell us about a topic or idea that excites you and is related to one or more academic areas you selected above. Why are you drawn to it? (200 words or fewer) In this essay, it’s critical that your answer aligns with your past activities and showcases your curious nature in your spare time. It’s fine to go deep here with the answer and nerd out!
- slide_04: 3. What is it about Yale that has led you to apply? You should reacher on alumni boards or student forums particular aspects about Yale that excite you. Name dropping professors or department isn’t good enough for this essay
- slide_05: 4. What inspires you? To impress AOs for this prompt mention an area of inspiration that’s unconventional to your daily why
- slide_06: 5. If you could teach any college course, write a book, or create an original piece of art of any kind, what would it be? For this essay, you can give an out of the box answer that others will not mention. The more whimsical the better
- slide_07: 6. Other than a family member, who is someone who has had a significant influence on you? An out of the box standout answer is mentioning someone from fiction/podcast/ history etc. Generic students will mention a coach or teacher
- slide_08: 7 . What is something about you that is not included anywhere else in your application? You could mention a unique hobby here that somewhat relates to your hook
- slide_09: 8.Reflect on a time you discussed an issue important to you with someone holding an opposing view. Why did you find the experience meaningful? This essay is utilized to show diversity and flexibility of thought , don’t be afraid to be controversial here
- slide_10: 9.Reflect on your membership ina community to which you feel connected. Why is this community meaningful to you? You may define community however you like. This essay is the backdoor diversity essay. Mention your race here if URM. This essay is also for different viewpoints or orientations
- slide_11: 10. Reflect on an element of your personal experience that you feel will enrich your college. How has it shaped you? This essay is straightforward. You should write here the best way you can impact the Yale community
