→  That reduced how much I needed to study after school.

→ This gave my brain permission to rest and reset instead of obsessing over what I didn’t understand.

→ Instead of always reviewing in the same spot, I rotated between 2 or 3 different locations like my desk, the kitchen table, even my backyard.
