Does GPA or SAT scores matter more? GPA, but not because of the number. A 3.7 with five APs is worth more than a 4.0 with zero because colleges read your transcript in the context of what your school offers. Your SAT confirms what your grades already say, and a strong score can make up for a lower GPA because it proves you can perform at that level even if your grades don't fully show it. But a high GPA with a low SAT may indicate inconsistency. Rule thumb with SAT is to aim at or above your target school's median score.
