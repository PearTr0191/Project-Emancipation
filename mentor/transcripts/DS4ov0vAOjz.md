- slide_01: How to write the Harvard Essays
- slide_02: 1) Question: Harvard has long recognized the importance of enrolling a diverse student body. How will the life experiences that shape who you ate today enable you to contribute to Harvard? (10-150 words) Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): ¢ This prompt was added after the supreme court ruling on race not being a factor in admissions. ¢ If you’re a URM, you should be writing about your diverse background. ¢ Otherwise, focus on an experience that gave you a diverse perspective ¢ Make sure to explain how that diverse perspective will help you contribute to the campus at Harvard.
- slide_03: 2) Question: Describe a time when you strongly disagreed with someone about an idea or issue. How did you communicate ot engage with this person? What did you learn from this experience? Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): ¢ This prompt is about being able to learn from someone that you disagree with ¢ This prompt is meant to weed out students who are not able to discuss sensitive topics without getting out of control ¢ You should discuss a time when you ere able to engage in a productive way with someone you disagreed with and learned something
- slide_04: 3) Question: Briefly describe any of your extracurricular activities, employment experience, travel, or family responsibilities that have shaped who you ate. (10-150 words) Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): ¢ For this, Ivy Brothers recommends either picking an activity that you didn’t talk about much in the rest of your application, OR one of your top activities that is so great that you need to elaborate on it.
- slide_05: 4) Question: How do you hope to use your Harvard education in the future? (10-150 words) Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): ¢ You should have a super big moonshot goal here (don’t just say you want to work in investment banking). ¢ You should have a goal in mind that is extremely lofty and awe-inspiring.
- slide_06: 5) Question: Top 3 things your roommates might like to know about you. (10-150 words) Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): ¢ Pick 3 very quirky or niche things that are admirable and will show your personality ¢ If you choose things like “ambitious” or “meticulous,” you failed at this prompt.
