## Slide text

- slide_01: IVY BROTHERS HOW TO WRITE THE STANFORD ESSAYS - ADMISSION OFFICER INSIDER
- slide_02: Short Prompts 1) Question: What is the most significant challenge that society faces today? Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): Don’t pick common issues like economic inequality or climate change. Pick something thousands applicants won’t write about. For example, you can discuss how X resource is about to run out and no one is paying attention. Society can’t function without X resource
- slide_03: 2) Question: How did you spend your last two summets? Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): No admissions does not cate about your family trip to Greece. You can come off as too well off. You should write here about your spike and some impact related thing you did, which can potentially be continued at Stanford
- slide_04: 3) Question: What historical moment or event do you wish you could have witnessed? Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): This is an intellectual curiosity essay. Write about something here admission officers haven’t heard like the discovery or X or the first ____ that set the foundation for a modern way of work
- slide_05: 4) Question: Briefly elaborate on one of your extracurricular activities, a job you hold, or responsibilities you have for your family. Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): List something that relates to your spike but is not present in the rest of your ECs
- slide_06: 5) Question: List five things that are important to you. Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): Put eccentric answers or answers related to your spike ot Stanford’s initiatives. Eccentric answers do better
- slide_07: Long Prompts 1) Question: The Stanford community is deeply curious and driven to learn in and out of the classroom. Reflect on an idea or experience that makes you genuinely excited about learning. Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): This is an intellectual curiosity essay, you can talk about a research you did or a topic you love to deep drive into. College wants to make sure you love learning
- slide_08: Long Prompts 2) Question: Virtually all of Stanford’s undergraduates live on campus. Write a note to your future roommate that reveals something about you or that will help your roommate—and us—get to know you better. Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): This is an essay to see what you would be like to the student body. A good way to answer this essay “I can’t way to stay up till 3am discussing quantum mechanics and going to the blank building for a midnight snack. Looking forward to going to _____ Stanford event together as well”
- slide_09: Long Prompts 3) Question: Please describe what aspects of your life experiences, interests and character would help you make a distinctive contribution as an undergraduate to Stanford University. Answer (Ivy Brothers Lens): This is the hidden diversity essay. You can mention here your cultural experience or other diversity factors. If not diverse demographically you can be diverse in experience and what you might contribute to the campus
