## Caption

I know you’re probably thinking, “That’s easy for you to say Elise.”

Yes, I got into Harvard, built a collective following of 2M+, was featured on Forbes, and invited to speak at multiple Harvard conferences.

But honestly, when I was 16, I wanted all of that because I wanted other people to validate that I was worth something…

What I figured out when I reached these goals is that the moments I actually cared about had nothing to do with any of it. 

They were the times I got to show up for someone, or build something that positively impacted the people around me. 

That’s when I understood that what matters most to me is community and service, and that none of it was going to mean much until it was focused at something bigger than myself.

So I’ve stopped measuring my life by the goals I set at 16.

I’m proud of that girl and everything she worked for…

But I’m chasing something different now, and it doesn’t depend on tangible success or anyone else’s approval.

My mission for @ultimateivyleagueguide was never just about getting into college. It’s about helping students see everything they’re capable of, and walking them through a process that feels scary so they never have to face it alone ❤️

The acceptance letters tend to follow:)
