- slide_02: Deferral vs rejected * Let's cut the pleasantries. If you opened your portal this week and didn't see confetti, you are likely hearing a lot of comforting noises right now. * Your guidance counselor is telling you "it's not a no, it's a maybe." * Your parents are saying “it's their Loss." - slide_03: Different types deferrals * Yield Protection Deferral: They suspect they are your backup school. They are waiting to see if you will beg via a Letter of Continued Interest. The backup student deferral: They filled their athletic and donor spots. You are a backup option on the shelf in case they can't find a better version of you in March. The Courtesy Deferral: Common at schools like Harvard and Georgetown. They defer a lot students to keep high school counselors happy. It signals: "decent, but we don't want you."
- slide_04: What to do if deferred * You should make slight changes and see how you can become more unique if you et deferred. More than likely the college elieved you weren't top tier/unique enough for a superstar class. * For the RD round make slight changes to have a more unique profile.
- slide_05: Real reason a college rejects a student * A rejection is market feedback. It means you weren't in the top 20% of candidates (excluding colleges such as Harvard where everyone is deferred). It's market feedback that something in your application is not up to par. * You should make significant changes if you get rejected from your target university.
- slide_06: 5 reasons why students get rejected : Tie seen Cut: Your numbers didn't survive the first read. The Red Flag: If your stats were perfect but you were rejected, you signaled danger.
