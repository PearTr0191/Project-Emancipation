## Caption

Most students try to “stand out” by joining every club possible.

But here’s the truth: Colleges look for if you have one passion-driven extracurricular that has trajectory, depth, and personal alignment. 

If you want to build a passion project that actually gets noticed, use this 5 STEP DOMINO FRAMEWORK 🎯

🔷 Step 1: Answer the FQO (Frustration, Question, Obsession)
The best projects don’t start with “what looks good.”
They start with what feels real.
→ What problem do you wish you could solve?
→ What question won’t leave your head?
→ What niche topic could you deep-dive into for hours?

🔷 Step 2: Launch the MVA
Test the waters with a simple, low-lift action:
→ Host a small event or workshop
→ Post one article or short-form video
→ Interview a few experts and publish takeaways

This is your Minimum Viable Action (proof that your idea has legs)

🔷 Step 3: Build Credibility Bridges
Your next move: amplify reach and legitimacy.
→ Partner with a local org or mentor
→ Publish your work (school newspaper, Medium, local news)
→ Turn impact into a visual asset (PDF toolkit, explainer video, infographic)

This is how you build something worth sharing and remembering.

🔷 Step 4: Connect the Academic Dots
A project means nothing if it doesn’t go anywhere.
→ What did this teach you?
→ How does it shape your future goals?
→ How will you expand this in college?

This is your “why it matters” moment.

🔷 Step 5: Brand It Like You Mean It
→ Give your project a real name
→ Create a one-pager (use Carrd or Notion)
→ Make it linkable in essays or your activities list

If it looks real, it becomes real to admissions officers, mentors, and scholarship reviewers.

📍 EXAMPLE: 
→ My friend grew up translating at doctor visits for her immigrant parents (Step 1)
→ Created bilingual flyers explaining health terms (Step 2)
→ Partnered with a clinic to share them (Step 3)
→ Realized her interest in public health (Step 4)
→ Built a simple site so others could access the resources (Step 5)
