⬇️Why your goals may be holding you back

✍🏻Setting goals is a great why to maintain your ambition and motivation. 

⭐️However, if you make your goal (academic, financial, social, etc.) the THRESHOLD for your happiness, you will always keep chasing. 

📆Oftentimes, students tell me me that life feels so repetitive and that they keep thinking about *the next step* — even to the point that they become unmotivated from the chase. 

🧠Shift your mindset to focus on your day-to-day habits and tasks. If you know you want to become a better student, do something every day that will contribute to that (such as learning a study technique) rather than saying “I have to get ___ grade or else I’m a failure.”

✅In the end, you’ll surpass your goals anyways because the new habits you’ve made are sustainable.
