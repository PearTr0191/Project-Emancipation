- slide_02: Prompt Answers 1) Question: “Describe an example of your leadership experience in which you have positively influenced others, helped resolve disputes or contributed to group efforts over time.” * Pick this prompt if: you have a leadership role that’s unique. It doesn’t have to be club leadership, it can be leading a field trip or mentoring youth, or leading an online group of 1000 people * Ivy Brothers perspective: we recommend using this prompt to share a short story about how you helped a group. The admission officers do not want to read 1000 model UN or sport stories. Think outside the box on how your leadership positively impacted a group
- slide_03: Prompt Answers 2) Question: “Every person has a creative side, and it can be expressed in many ways: problem solving, original and innovative thinking, and artistically, to name a few. Describe how you express your creative side.” * Pick this prompt if: you can use it showcase your intellectual curiosity or quirkiness * Ivy Brothers perspective: write here about helping fund a school club with limited resources, cooking a meal with limited ingredients, or mention new skills you learned to play an instrument or do art better
- slide_04: Prompt Answers 3) Question: “What would you say is your greatest talent or skill? How have you developed and demonstrated that talent over time?” * Pick this prompt if: you have an X factor skill not many students who are applying to UCs have * Ivy Brothers perspective: describe how the skill originated then go into detail about how you used it overtime. Be very passionate in this essay to show your love for this skill
