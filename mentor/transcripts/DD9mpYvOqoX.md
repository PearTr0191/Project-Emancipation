## Caption

It’s not about cramming in every activity possible— it’s about creating a clear, connected story that reflects who you are.

1️⃣ COHERENCE IS KEY: Every piece of your application—essays, activities, letters, and even your passion project—should work together to show your values, interests, and impact. Admissions officers want to see the bigger picture.

2️⃣ LEADERSHIP + GROWTH MINDSET: It’s not just about what you’ve achieved—it’s about how you’ve grown and what drives you. What lessons have shaped you? How do you plan to build on them in college?

3️⃣ USE A PASSION PROJECT TO ANCHOR YOUR STORY: Think of it as the centerpiece of your application that connects everything else.

📌 Example: If you’ve started a tutoring program for underserved students, here’s how it ties together:
→ Essays: Share the “why” behind the project—maybe a personal story about barriers you faced in your education.
→ Activities list: Highlight the measurable impact (e.g., 50+ students reached, improved test scores).
→ Recommendation letters: Teachers mention your initiative and leadership in starting and managing the program.
→ Supplements: Tie your project to your future goals—like creating equity in education or studying public policy.

When everything clicks, your application tells a story that’s impossible to ignore 🌟
