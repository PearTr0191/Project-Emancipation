## Caption

Here’s the biggest mistake students make ⬇️

They think getting into top schools like NYU is about stacking more APs, clubs, and “impressive” titles.

But when you look at accepted vs rejected students, the difference is usually something way simpler…

The students who get in have a clear direction in their classes and activities. You can tell what they care about just skimming their application.

The students who get rejected often did everything right on paper… but nothing connects.

If you don’t build a theme until senior year, you’re stuck forcing a story out of whatever you already did ❌

If you start earlier, your choices get easier. You know what to say yes to, and you stop wasting time on things that don’t help your story ✅
