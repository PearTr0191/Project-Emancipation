1. College Grading System
I took every college on my list and rated
them on a scale of 1-10 for...
- Academic fit for my major
- Cultural fit for my major
- Can I see myself happy at school
Every school that wasn't an 8/10 or
higher in all categories was disqualified.

---

2. Financial Aid Programs
I wasn't blessed with an infinite budget
for school so I had to make ends meet.
Every college needed...
- Financial aid (min 25% of tuition)
- Strong scholarship support
- Clear path to jobs/internships
This way I wouldn't graduate from a
prestigious university with a prestigious
amount of debt
