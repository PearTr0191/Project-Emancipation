What makes a good essay hook?
Sets the tone
Points to a bigger truth
Aligns with your core message
Raises curiosity
Keeps the story flowing naturally
What makes a bad essay hook?
Detached
Unoriginal
Leans on clichés
Lacks connection

Uncoventional Reality Hook
Accepted
"'ve saved the town 35 times, but every time I did, I lost
my sister."
Why it works:
Creates curiosity with an unexpected twist.
Rejected
"I was the lead in my school's play, and my sister also
had a major role. It was one of the most memorable
experiences of my life."
Why it doesn't work:
reason tokeep reading.

---

Dialogue Hook
Accepted
"My mother always told me, ^The warmth of your hands
is what makes each loaf special.'I didn't understand
until I realized she wasn't talking about temperature."
Why it works:
Uses a personal detail to hint at a deeper meaning.
Rejected
"Family is the most important ingredient in life. Love is
the secret ingredient in baking.
Whyitdoesn'twork:
Uses a common metaphorinstead of telling a
personal story.

---

Vivid Imagery
Accepted
"The chandelier was still swinging when I opened my
notebook. Around me, people screamed for the
stairwell. I started counting the seconds between
aftershocks."
Why it works:
Drops the reader into an unexpected moment
through contrast.
Rejected
"I played Dungeons & Dragons with my friends every
Friday evening."
Whyitdoesn'twork:
Describesaroutineinsteadofanactivemoment.

---

Unconventional Reality:
Accepted
"I hate seeing blood in vampire movies. Somehow, I
found myself surrounded by it every Friday at 4pm.'
Why it works:
Reveals a contradiction that sparks curiosity.
Rejected
"Volunteering at a blood drive was a meaningful
experience that taught me the importance of helping
others."
Why itdoesn'twork:
Could apply to any volunteer activity and reveals the
lesson too early.
