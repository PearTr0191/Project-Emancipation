## Caption

One of the most COMMON mistakes that gets students rejected from their dream college is not knowing how to answer school-specific interview questions ⬇️

Here’s how each answer differs: 

❌Rejected Student: Nothing here explains why NYU specifically, why this student, or how NYC connects to their lived experiences or goals. Admissions can swap “NYU” with 50 other schools and lose nothing.

⚠️Waitlisted student: This shows awareness of NYU’s setting, but the motivation stays abstract. “Internships,” “real-world experiences,” and “interdisciplinary” are signals of interest, but NYU still doesn’t know who this student is or what they’ll actually do.

✅ Accepted student: This answer connects past → present → future:
	•	Past: a concrete, personal experience tied to NYU
	•	Present: a clear academic direction aligned with NYU
	•	Future: a specific way the student intends to engage with the school’s environment

When you answer school-specific interview questions, don’t explain why the school is impressive…explain why your story would unfold differently there than anywhere else.
