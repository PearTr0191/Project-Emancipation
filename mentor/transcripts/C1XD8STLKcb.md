## Caption

The reality of common habits 🧠⬇️ 

1. Re-writing your notes

- Note-taking is a passive action that creates an illusion of productivity. In reality, testing your ability to recall information is the key to forming neural connections. 

2. Re-reading your textbook

- Avoid doing this as your main method of studying for an exam. It’s a low-utility action that doesn’t allow you to discover your knowledge gaps. 

3. Pulling all-nighters

- Deep rest builds neuroplasticity. Despite studying hard, if you don’t sleep enough, your ability to memorize information long-term is reduced. 

4. Ignoring your biological cycle

- Everyone’s peak productivity time differs. Identify yours and set aside that period of time for intense study sessions. Trying to study hard when your body isn’t at its peak leads to unproductive work.
