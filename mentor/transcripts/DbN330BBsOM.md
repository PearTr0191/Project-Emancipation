## Caption

Most students think a “Why This Major?” essay is about how much you love the subject 😬

The thing that actually separates a rejected, waitlisted, and accepted answer is your trajectory.

Here’s why each response got its result:

❌ Rejected: Leans on clichés about the field. It’s the future, there’s so much opportunity, and I’ve always loved it….every single applicant to the major could write the same lines.

⚠️ Waitlisted: Shows real interest and a bit of research, naming AI and a class or two. The answer still sits in the abstract, with plenty about what the field offers and not much about the student’s own work.

✅ Accepted: Connects three things: something the student already built, the insight she pulled from it, and where she’ll take it next. The major becomes the next step in a story that’s already moving.
