What is the Common App Essay?
Your Essay isn't
what you think it is.
Your Common App essay is not a list of achievements.
It's not about proving you're the smartest student in the
room, and it's definitely not a brag sheet.
Your essay is a reflection of who you are and how you've grown.
A strong personal statement answers:
Whathappened?
What did it mean to you?
Why did it matter?
