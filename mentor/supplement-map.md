## Supplement strategy overview

Each school gets 1–2 supplements (varies by school). The strategy is: **one Core Value + one Theme + one Asset (ViDrive) deployed differently per school**, aligned to what the school's C7 says it weighs. The Triangle Rule and PPF framework (see knowledge/common-app-essay.md) govern structure. The school's C7 grid (see knowledge/cds-comparison-9schools.md and knowledge/school-list-strategy.md) governs what to emphasize.

**Universal guardrails:**
- Specific beats general. Name programs, faculty, culture details. If an AO can swap your "NYU" for 50 other schools and lose nothing, the answer fails.
- The 5% Gap theme must do real work in every supplement — not just appear as a label.
- ViDrive is the anchor for the "builder" signal; other ECs (NoLlama merged PRs, GlowBal review work, Julience Science Festival, digital museum) are supporting. **Rescue assist is no longer usable as a supporting EC** — its Intel Festival elimination closed without advancing him (2026-10-02), so it carries no third-party validation and is out of Activities and Honors. Do not name it in a supplement.
- The "why us" component is non-negotiable. Generic "I'm excited by your vibrant campus" = instant reject.
